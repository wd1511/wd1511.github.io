I am a senior engineer in the Infrastructure Cloud Services Product Department at Huawei’s ICT Business Group. My research at Huawei focuses on world models for embodied AI and autonomous driving, as well as vision-language-action models. As part of my work on AI infrastructure, I have helped adapt DeepSeek V4, Kimi K3, Qwen2.5-VL, Wan2.1 and UniAD to the Ascend A5 platform. I also help build embodied agents and agents that support model adaptation. I earned my bachelor’s degree and PhD at Harbin Institute of Technology. My doctoral research, supervised by Professor Wen Gao, focused on image-to-image translation and vision-language models.
